Building GHData Market surfaced challenges around payments, trust and distribution that no amount
of planning fully prepared for.

A marketplace is mostly not a storefront. The storefront is the small part. The substantive product
is the transaction layer: reconciliation between payment providers, supplier APIs that occasionally
do not respond, inventory that must never be sold twice, and an agent network whose trust model the
platform has to encode carefully.

## Payments are a state machine

Every payment interaction is a conversation with an external system you do not control. The lesson
I keep re-learning: model every transaction as an explicit state machine, persist it, and make
retrying safe. Edge cases are not the tail of this system â€” they are a significant part of the
everyday operational load.

## Distribution is architecture too

An agent-based model is not just a go-to-market choice; it shapes the data model, the permission
system and the support burden. When you design the marketplace, you are designing the sales network.
