After completing a generative AI and prompt engineering programme, here is what has genuinely
changed in my day-to-day work — and what has not.

Generative tools have accelerated the parts of development that are well-specified: boilerplate,
test scaffolding, explaining unfamiliar code, drafting documentation. They have not changed the
hard part — knowing what to build, understanding the operational context, and judging whether the
output is actually correct for the situation.

## What has genuinely changed

- Faster iteration on the mechanical layer of a task.
- Better exploration of options when I am unsure how to approach something.
- A useful second pair of eyes for reviewing code I did not write recently.

## What has not

- Requirement gathering. Talking to the school, the clinic, the marketplace operator still matters
  more than any model can.
- Architectural judgement under real constraints — deployment environment, data reliability, who
  actually maintains the system afterwards.
- Accountability. A wrong answer from a model is still my bug to fix.
